DeepThought — Child Safety Standards

Draft — lawyer review pending before launch.
Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Our position — zero tolerance

1.1 DeepThought has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form.

1.2 Any sexual content involving a minor, and any content that sexualises a minor in any way, is strictly prohibited (Community Rules, Section 7.2(a)). It is stopped before publishing where the safety filter catches it, and where it is reported or found, it is acted on at the highest priority — before every other kind of moderation work.

1.3 Adults sometimes write honestly about difficult experiences, including things that happened when they were young. Writing about your own life is welcome here. Sexual content involving a minor is not — the line is absolute and there are no exceptions, warnings, or second chances for it.

2. DeepThought is for adults only

2.1 DeepThought is an 18+ service. You must be 18 years or older to use it (Terms of Service, Section 3; Community Rules, Section 9.1). The app asks every new user to confirm they are 18 or older before entering.

2.2 No part of DeepThought is directed at children. There is no children's content, no children's section, and nothing in the app is designed to appeal to anyone under 18.

2.3 If we learn that an account belongs to a person under 18, we close that account and remove its content (Privacy Policy, Section 13.2). If you believe a person under 18 is using DeepThought, please report it through the app or write to the contact in Section 6 below.

3. How the app is built to reduce this risk

3.1 Text only. DeepThought is a writing app: users cannot post photos, videos, voice notes, or files of any kind. There is no image or video sharing anywhere in the service.

3.2 No private contact. There is no direct messaging, no chat, and no way for one user to contact another privately. Users cannot share links or contact details in posts (Community Rules, Section 4).

3.3 Everything is checked before it appears. Every post, thought, echo, book, and ripple passes a safety filter before publishing: writing that clearly breaks the Community Rules never goes live; borderline writing is held, invisible to readers, until a human reviewer at our moderation Desk decides.

3.4 Readers can report, and reports have teeth. Every post, thought, echo, book, Ripple chain, and individual Ripple part carries a Report control. If three different readers report the same live content, it is hidden automatically and sent to the Desk for a human decision (Community Rules, Section 11.3).

4. How to report child-safety concerns

4.1 In the app: use the Report control on the content itself. Child-safety reports are handled at the highest priority by the Desk.

4.2 By email: write to our designated child-safety point of contact — DeepThought Helpline, deepthoughthelpline@gmail.com. This inbox is also our published grievance contact (Privacy Policy, Section 14). Grievances are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 7 days; a grievance asking us to remove content that breaks the Community Rules is decided within 36 hours.

4.3 You do not need a DeepThought account to email us about a child-safety concern.

5. What we do when we find or are told about CSAE

5.1 Remove and stop. The content is removed, and the account that posted it is stopped: a stopped account cannot post, and its content is removed from readers' view (Community Rules, Sections 11.4–11.5).

5.2 Report to the authorities. Confirmed child sexual abuse material is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and/or to India's I4C National Cybercrime Reporting Portal, as applicable to the case. Our written internal protocol for this is kept by the owner and followed in every case.

5.3 Cooperate with lawful requests. Where a court or an authorised government agency lawfully requires information or assistance, we provide it within the period the IT Rules, 2021 prescribe (Privacy Policy, Section 12.4).

5.4 No public shaming. Enforcement is private. We never publish the identity of a reporter, and we never announce punishments to readers (Community Rules, Section 11.6).

6. Point of contact

6.1 Designated child-safety point of contact: DeepThought Helpline — deepthoughthelpline@gmail.com (responsible person: Daksh, Grievance Officer).

6.2 This contact can be used by users, parents, authorities, and Google Play / Apple reviewers for any child-safety question or report about DeepThought.

7. Review of these standards

7.1 These standards are published to meet Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and Apple's child-safety requirements, and they describe practices that are already in force in the app today.

7.2 If these standards change, the new version will be published on this page before it applies.
